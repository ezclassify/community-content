# Community Schemes Changelog

All notable changes to the community scheme catalog are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0]

The first public release of the community scheme catalog, published as
`@ezclassify/schemes`. Open-source marking schemes for EzClassify under
Apache-2.0: three drawn from public standards, the Traffic Light Protocol
(`tlp`), CVSS v3.1 (`cvss`), and CVSS v4.0 (`cvss-v4`), and three fictional
Cedar Valley teaching schemes (`cedar-valley-protection`,
`cedar-valley-handling`, and `cedar-valley`) with their guides and access
policies. Schemes ship compiled under `dist/schemes/`. Every scheme carries
test suites in the `schemes.ezclassify.com/v1` suite format, compiled under
`dist/test-suites/`.
